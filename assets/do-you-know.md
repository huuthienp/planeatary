# DO YOU KNOW?

Our global food system is contributing approximately one third of
greenhouse gas emissions.&nbsp;[^1]

Experts have called for an urgent transformation to the way food is produced and consumed,
including a population-wide shift to healthy and sustainable diets.&nbsp;[^2]

The PlanEATary Quest is a fun, interactive intervention that aims to help you:

- Identify how you can improve the way you're interacting with our food
  system to promote human and planetary health.
- Adopt new healthy and environmentally sustainable food habits,
  inspired by a supportive and collegial approach.
- Reflect on the factors that help or hinder your environmentally
  sustainable food habits.
